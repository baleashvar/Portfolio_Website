Interview Insights

Users are now moving back or have just moved back to the cities from their hometowns as the pandemic has started to fade away

The main reason for choosing PG is to have a safe which is more preffered by the college students and just starting to work profesionals. People choosing shared accomidation is to share the financial burden and some people also do feel sharing makes a better bond with people leading to great friendships and memories.

People have changed couple of accomidations varying on so many factors ranging from job changing to a different location in same city, the better financially stable they become the better the place they look for to live, couple of problem caused by the roommate.

Idea accomidation for almost every user is private room with attached bathroom in their budget that is also close to their office/college.

Idea roomate is a very like minded person person with similar interests, having good values/ethics also valuing each person’s privacy.

Users have choose their current roomate from either friends or family connections but in which case they do sometime have to sacrifice basic things from their ideal room as they did not know any other means. Or they do it with their collegues from the office after a couple of months after joining the company.

Only 1 out of 5 users knew a roommate searching platform. 3 were aware of colive and zolo but which is just a PG
