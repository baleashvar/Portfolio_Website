Play .

search, play and chill.

Play, an app to suggest movie/tv show to users, depending on the likes of theirs at that moment with family or friends and many more. And also to help the user to keep a track of movies that they have seen and to see people’s reviews of the movie/tv show along with the critics reviews. And also to help users find the platform where the movie is on

This project was submited for “Interaction design” in my college.

Time: JAN 2021 - APR 2021

Focus group .

Interview Insights

Watching movies on a regular interval varies from people to people, but mostly its 2 or 3 movies a week especially after lockdown.


People who are more movie buff watch more movies often then the not so movie buff ones as expected.



More people have actually brought amazon prime, Netflix or other streaming services after lockdown



“To watch” list actually only followed by people who watch more than 3 movies a week. The lesser ones tend to randomly select the movie that comes to their mind first.



People actually face this problem of not knowing what to watch. And many times they just tend to watch a classic or a old personal favorite movie of theirs.



And watching movies actually helps people, like many of the people when they are feeling low they watch the “tom hanks” movies which could lift their spirit up.



If a person wants to watch horror and have seen all the main stream ones, they just go to google or youtube searching for underrated horror movies to find the nice ones.

Survey involved over 50 people ranging from school students to working people and moviebuffs to barely watchers, asking them the basic questions regarding the problem to get an overview a collective group and issues that are faced by them.

Different people have different taste on movies. One guys all time favorite may be other guys worst of all time.



The views on comedies however doesn’t change as mostly everyone had similar favorite comedy movies.



Even the movies that close friends suggest might not be favorite for a person



Finding the Streaming service of a movie is annoying



Movie with high critics rating may not be very good for general audience.

Focus group was conducted with 4 people, again ranging from active movie watchers (3-4 a week) to once in a time watchers (2 weeks once).

Key Insights

Users many times want to watch movies depending on how they are feeling at the right moment rather than completing movies on their watchlist

Movies with just good reviews may not be good enough to suggest to people rather it is more of the genre itself

Many time get stuck on what to watch at the moment

Almost 88% of people have got stuck on what to watch

Almost 73% of people have have seen a movie that has high ratings but did not like

Almost 90% of people don’t have a proper place to keep a watchlist

Almost 30% of people ask google for movie suggestions, the rest 30% to friends and 40% through social media
