Time Pickle is a time management mobile application that lets the user schedule their days, weeks. It is mainly aimed at the young professionals who are or just have moved into their new job. We at time pickle analyse the maintaince of your work life balance and give away rewards based on your perfectly accomplished day.

This project was done during the Design Sprint in the training period at COGNIZANT

Time: 13th - 15th JULY 2022
Team Members: Preena, Ashutosh, Jayanarayan, Subhangi, Srishti and Devunath

HMW help young professionals manage their time?

After watching couple of expert talks on the topic, me and my teammates came up with list of “HOW MIGHT WE” solve the problem. After coming up with the questions, we categroiezed it through affinity clustering and voted on the main topic to be worked on.

Each of the team member came up with their own customer journey and again the most important points and the best possible journey was put togeather to find what, where and how the user is facing the problem.

Each team member had to come with the 8 craziest idea to solve the problem under 8 mins. And after which again the best one would be voted and focused upon.

Each team member had to come up with a possible solution method using the reward system selected in the past round. Which again, will be voted and selected.

Each team member found the features from other applications that would be helpful in this problem.

Similar to the journey map, each team member came up with their own story and the best situations was voted and picked, put togeather.
